Road traffic crash is a growing and alarming concern for the major highways in Bangladesh. This study investigates the factors influencing traffic crash severity along the Hatikumrul interchange to Rangpur segment of the N5 National Highway, a major national corridor connecting Dhaka with northern Bangladesh by using machine learning techniques. After pre-processing, a raw dataset of 480 accident records was obtained from the Micro-Computer Accident Analysis Package (MAAP-5) maintained by the Accident Research Institute (ARI)
